Trade Clips

Turns a chart recording and a face recording of one live trade into Reece's vertical live-trade clip.

Install guide (PDF)

Install it

About two minutes, once.

  1. Open Trade-Clips-0.2.0-arm64.dmg from your Downloads folder. A small window opens.
  2. Drag the Trade Clips icon onto the Applications folder in that window.
  3. Open your Applications folder and double-click Trade Clips.

The first time only: let your Mac open it

Your Mac may say it can't check Trade Clips for malware. That's expected: Reece made it himself, and it isn't from the App Store.

  1. Click Done on that message (not Move to Bin).
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, then scroll down on the right.
  3. Next to “Trade Clips was blocked…”, click Open Anyway. Enter your Mac password if it asks, then click Open Anyway again.

That's it. From now on Trade Clips opens like any other app.

Still won't open?

If it keeps refusing, or the first export says FFmpeg can't start, open the Terminal app, paste this line, press Return, then open Trade Clips again.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Trade Clips.app"

Making a clip

Click New Clip, add the chart and face recordings of the trade, then follow the steps on the left: Recordings, Line Up, Layout, Mark Trade, Export. When a step is done, the blue button at the top right moves you on. Everything saves by itself, and the app works offline with no account.

The video engine inside Trade Clips is FFmpeg, which is free software under the GNU GPL. Its source code is on the release page. You don't need it to use the app.
